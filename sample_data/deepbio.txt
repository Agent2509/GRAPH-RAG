DeepBio is a biotechnology startup based in Zurich, founded by molecular biologist Dr. Marcus Smith in 2019. DeepBio specializes in computational immunology and automated protein design.

In early 2023, Dr. Marcus Smith and his research team at DeepBio engineered CureX, a synthetic peptide targeting aggressive solid tumors. Recognizing the massive synergy between artificial intelligence and biological research, NexusLabs acquired DeepBio in November 2023 for $320 million. Following the acquisition, NexusLabs deployed CortexGraph to simulate protein-protein docking interactions for CureX, accelerating its preclinical phase by over eighteen months.
